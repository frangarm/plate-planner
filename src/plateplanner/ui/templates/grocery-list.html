How It Works

Plate Planner uses your weekly meal plan to create a grocery list.

  • Ingredients are collected from your planned recipes.
  • Quantities are adjusted based on the number of servings.
  • Matching ingredients are combined when their units are compatible.
  • You can update quantities, remove items, and mark items as purchased.
